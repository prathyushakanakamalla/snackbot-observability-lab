# Warehouse Operations Handbook

Revision 2023-B — Owner: DC Operations. (Scheduled for review.)

## Receiving
1. Check the bill of lading against the purchase order.
2. Inspect pallets for damage, pests, and broken seals.
3. For refrigerated loads, record the trailer temperature. Reject the load if the product
   temperature is **above 5 °C (41 °F)**.
4. Scan every pallet into the warehouse management system (WMS) within 1 hour of receipt.

## Storage zones
- **Zone A** — ambient snacks (chips, popcorn, salsa)
- **Zone B** — beverages (Fizzwell); max stack height 4 pallets
- **Zone C** — refrigerated cooler for DipCo dips
- **Zone Q** — QA Hold cage (locked; QA staff only)

## Picking
Pick by FEFO. Pickers must scan both the location label and the pallet label.

## Forklift safety
- Only certified operators may drive forklifts. Certification is renewed every **3 years**.
- Speed limit inside the warehouse: **8 km/h (5 mph)**.
- Pedestrians must stay in marked walkways.

## Shipping
Outbound trailers are sealed and the seal number is recorded on the bill of lading.
Refrigerated trailers must be pre-cooled to 2 °C before loading.
