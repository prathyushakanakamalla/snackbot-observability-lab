# Consumer Complaints — Customer Care FAQ

Owner: Consumer Affairs. Last reviewed August 2026.

## How consumers contact us
Phone 1-800-555-0199 (Mon–Fri, 8am–8pm ET) or the web form on our site.
Target first response time: **24 hours** for web forms.

## Common complaints and responses

**"The bag is half empty."**
Chips settle during shipping; bags are filled by weight, not volume. If the net weight is below the
declared amount, offer a replacement coupon and log a Class 3 quality incident.

**"I found something in my product."**
Ask for the best-by date and lot code (printed on the back of the bag near the top), a photo, and
whether anyone was hurt. Ask the consumer to keep the product. Log in the QMS and escalate to Quality
per SOP-QI-007. **Never** tell a consumer the product is safe before Quality has reviewed.

**"I had an allergic reaction."**
Treat as potential Class 1. Express concern, recommend they seek medical attention, collect lot code,
and escalate to Quality immediately (within 2 hours).

**"Where can I buy Jalapeño Kick?"**
It was discontinued in June 2025. Suggest Crunchwell BBQ Smokehouse as an alternative.

## Compensation guidelines
- Standard: replacement coupon for one product (up to $6 value).
- Agents may issue up to **$25** in coupons without supervisor approval.
- Never offer cash or admit liability. Legal questions go to the Legal team.
