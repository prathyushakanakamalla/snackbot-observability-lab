# SOP-FS-001: Food Safety Standard Operating Procedure

Version 4.2 — effective March 1, 2026. Owner: Quality & Food Safety (Dr. Priya Natarajan).

## 1. Scope
Applies to all three manufacturing plants and all four distribution centers.

## 2. Temperature control
| Product type | Storage temperature | Max time outside range |
|---|---|---|
| Refrigerated dips (DipCo) | 0 °C to 4 °C (32 °F to 39 °F) | 30 minutes |
| Chips, popcorn, salsa (ambient) | 10 °C to 27 °C (50 °F to 80 °F) | Not critical |
| Fizzwell beverages | 2 °C to 30 °C | Avoid freezing |

If a refrigerated product is outside 0–4 °C for **more than 30 minutes**, it must be placed on
**QA Hold** and the shift QA lead must be notified immediately. Product on QA Hold cannot be shipped.

## 3. Hand washing and hygiene
- Wash hands for at least 20 seconds before entering a production zone.
- Hair nets and beard nets are required in all production zones.
- Jewelry (except a plain wedding band) is not permitted in production zones.

## 4. Metal detection
Every finished-goods line has an inline metal detector. Test the detector with certified test pieces
(ferrous 1.5 mm, non-ferrous 2.0 mm, stainless 2.5 mm) at the start of each shift and every **2 hours**.
If a test fails, all product since the last successful test must be placed on QA Hold and re-screened.

## 5. Fryer oil
Fryer oil total polar compounds (TPC) must be checked every 4 hours. Replace oil when TPC reaches **24%**.

## 6. Sanitation
Full clean-in-place (CIP) of dip lines after every production run and at least every 24 hours.
Allergen changeover cleaning is covered in POL-AL-002 (Allergen Control Policy).

## 7. Records
All temperature, metal-detector, and oil-quality records are kept for **2 years**.
