# HR-POL-03: Vacation & Leave Policy

Effective July 1, 2021. Owner: Human Resources.

## Vacation
Full-time employees receive vacation based on years of service:

| Years of service | Vacation days per year |
|---|---|
| Less than 5 years | 15 days |
| 5 years or more | 20 days |

- Up to **10 unused vacation days** may be carried over into the next year with no expiry.

## Sick leave
**6 paid sick days** per year.

## Parental leave
- Primary caregiver: **12 weeks** paid at 100%.
- Secondary caregiver: **4 weeks** paid at 100%.

## Requesting vacation
Email your manager at least 1 week in advance.
