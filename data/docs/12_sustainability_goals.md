# Sustainability Goals 2030

Published September 2025. Owner: Sustainability Office.

## Packaging
- **100% recyclable or compostable packaging by 2030** (2025 baseline: 61%).
- Reduce virgin plastic use by **40%** by 2030 versus 2022.
- Fizzwell cans already contain **70% recycled aluminum**.

## Climate
- Cut Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions by **50% by 2030** versus 2020.
- All three plants to run on **100% renewable electricity by 2028**. Fresno reached this in 2024.

## Water
- Reduce water use per tonne of product by **25%** by 2030 versus 2020.
- Fresno plant recycles fryer-wash water for cleaning floors and yards.

## Agriculture
- Source **100% of potatoes** from growers enrolled in our Regenerative Farming Program by 2030.
  In 2025, 38% of potatoes came from enrolled growers.

## Food waste
- Donate surplus edible product to food banks. In 2025 we donated **1.2 million pounds**.
