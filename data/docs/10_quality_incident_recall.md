# SOP-QI-007: Quality Incident & Recall Procedure

Version 3.4 — effective January 1, 2026. Owner: Quality & Food Safety.

## Incident classes
| Class | Definition | Examples | Escalate within |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | Reasonable probability of serious health harm | Undeclared allergen, foreign metal, Listeria/Salmonella positive | **2 hours** |
| Class 2 | Possible temporary or reversible health effect | Under-processed product, minor foreign material (plastic < 7 mm) | **24 hours** |
| Class 3 | Unlikely to cause harm; quality/regulatory | Wrong net weight, label typo without allergen impact | **5 business days** |

## Steps for any incident
1. **Contain**: Put all affected lots on QA Hold (physical + WMS hold).
2. **Notify**: Report to the plant QA manager and log the incident in the QMS (incident ID format QI-YYYY-NNNN).
3. **Investigate**: Trace affected lots one step back (suppliers) and one step forward (customers).
4. **Decide**: The Recall Committee decides whether to recall. For Class 1, the committee must meet
   within **4 hours** of escalation.
5. **Communicate**: Regulatory notification (FDA in the US, CFIA in Canada) within **24 hours** of a
   recall decision.

## Recall Committee
Head of Quality (chair), Chief Supply Chain Officer, General Counsel, VP Communications, and the plant manager.

## Mock recalls
Each plant runs a mock recall **twice per year**. Target: trace 100% of affected product within **4 hours**.
