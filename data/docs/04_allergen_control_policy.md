# POL-AL-002: Allergen Control Policy

Version 3.0 — effective January 1, 2026. Owner: Quality & Food Safety.

## Major allergens tracked
We track the "top 9" US major food allergens: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts,
peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame. Canada (Hamilton plant) also tracks mustard and sulphites.

## Peanuts and tree nuts
**No Crunchwell facility processes peanuts or tree nuts.** Ingredients containing peanuts or tree nuts
are not permitted on any site. Suppliers must certify their ingredients are produced in peanut-free
and tree-nut-free facilities.

## Production scheduling
Allergen-containing products run **last** in a production day, after allergen-free products.
Example order on a chips line: Sea Salt → Salt & Vinegar → BBQ (soy) → Sour Cream & Onion (milk).

## Changeover cleaning
After running a product containing an allergen, the line must receive a full wet clean followed by
an ATP swab test. ATP readings must be **below 150 RLU** before the next product can start.
A second person (QA technician) must verify and sign the changeover record.

## Labeling
Every allergen must appear in a "Contains:" statement on the package. Precautionary "may contain"
statements are only allowed when approved in writing by the Head of Quality.

## Allergen incidents
Any undeclared allergen found in finished product is a **Class 1 incident** and triggers the
recall evaluation process in SOP-QI-007 within 2 hours.
