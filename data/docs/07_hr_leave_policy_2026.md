# HR-POL-03: Paid Time Off & Leave Policy (CURRENT)

Effective January 1, 2026. This policy **replaces** all earlier versions. Owner: People Team.

## Paid time off (PTO)
PTO covers vacation and personal days. Full-time employees accrue PTO based on years of service:

| Years of service | PTO days per year |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 years | 20 days |
| 3 to 7 years | 23 days |
| More than 7 years | 26 days |

- Up to **5 unused PTO days** may be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days expire on **March 31** of the following year.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata.

## Sick leave
Separate from PTO: **10 paid sick days** per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer
than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave
- Birth parent: **16 weeks** fully paid.
- Non-birth parent (including adoptive parents): **12 weeks** fully paid.
- Must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement
Up to 5 paid days for an immediate family member; 2 days for extended family.

## Company holidays
11 company holidays per year (calendar published each December).

## Requesting leave
Submit PTO requests in Workday at least **2 weeks** in advance for absences of 3 days or more.
