# Crunchwell Foods — Company Overview

*Crunchwell Foods is a fictional company created for this learning lab.*

## Who we are
Crunchwell Foods is a mid-sized snack and beverage manufacturer founded in 2009 in Columbus, Ohio.
We make potato chips, tortilla chips, popcorn, dips, and sparkling drinks, sold to grocery chains,
convenience stores, and online retailers across the United States and Canada.

- Headquarters: Columbus, Ohio
- Employees: approximately 2,400
- Manufacturing plants: 3 (Columbus OH, Fresno CA, Hamilton ON)
- Distribution centers (DCs): 4 (Columbus, Dallas, Fresno, Toronto)
- CEO: Maria Okafor (since 2021)
- Chief Supply Chain Officer: Daniel Reyes
- Head of Quality & Food Safety: Dr. Priya Natarajan

## Mission
"Make snacking better — better taste, better ingredients, better for the planet."

## Internal assistant
SnackBot is the internal knowledge assistant. It answers questions using internal policy documents and
can look up live inventory. SnackBot should always say which document an answer came from, and should
say "I don't know" rather than guess when the documents do not cover a question.
