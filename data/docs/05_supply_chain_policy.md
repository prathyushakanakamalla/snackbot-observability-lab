# POL-SC-010: Supply Chain & Replenishment Policy

Version 2.1 — effective February 1, 2026. Owner: Daniel Reyes, Chief Supply Chain Officer.

## Reorder points
Each distribution center (DC) reorders a SKU when on-hand inventory falls to its reorder point:

**Reorder point = (average daily demand × lead time in days) + safety stock**

Safety stock is set to **7 days** of average daily demand for ambient products and **3 days**
for refrigerated products (because of shorter shelf life).

## Standard lead times (plant → DC)
| From plant | To DC | Lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Columbus | Columbus DC | 1 day |
| Columbus | Dallas DC | 3 days |
| Fresno | Fresno DC | 1 day |
| Fresno | Dallas DC | 4 days |
| Hamilton | Toronto DC | 1 day |
| Columbus | Toronto DC | 3 days (plus customs) |

Shipments to Canada must include a commercial invoice; add **1 extra day** for customs clearance.

## Stock rotation
All DCs use **FEFO** (first-expired, first-out). Product with less than **30% of its shelf life
remaining** must not be shipped to retail customers; it may be sold to the employee store or donated.

## Supplier rules
- All ingredient suppliers must be on the Approved Supplier List (ASL).
- New suppliers require a food-safety audit score of at least **85/100** before approval.
- Single-source ingredients must have a backup supplier identified within 6 months.

## Expediting
Expedited (air or team-driver) freight requires approval from a Supply Chain Director if the cost
exceeds **$5,000** per shipment.
