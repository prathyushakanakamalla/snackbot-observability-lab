# FIN-POL-07: Travel & Expense Policy

Version 5 — effective April 1, 2026. Owner: Finance.

## Booking
- Book all flights and hotels through the corporate travel portal (Navan).
- Economy class for flights under **6 hours**. Premium economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class requires VP approval.

## Hotels
Nightly hotel cap (before taxes):
| City tier | Examples | Cap per night |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | New York, San Francisco, Toronto | $275 |
| Tier 2 | Dallas, Chicago, Columbus | $190 |
| Tier 3 | All other cities | $150 |

## Meals
Daily meal allowance: **$75** per day in the US, **CAD $95** per day in Canada.
Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.

## Mileage
Personal car use is reimbursed at **$0.67 per mile**.

## Submitting expenses
- Submit expense reports in Concur within **30 days** of the trip end date.
- Receipts are required for any single expense of **$25 or more**.
- Reports over $2,500 need director approval.
