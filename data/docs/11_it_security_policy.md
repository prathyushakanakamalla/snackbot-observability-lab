# IT-POL-01: Information Security Policy (Employee Summary)

Version 2026.1 — Owner: IT Security.

## Passwords and sign-in
- Minimum password length: **14 characters**.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all company systems.
- Never share passwords, including with IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

## Data classification
| Level | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Press releases, website | No restrictions |
| Internal | Policies, org charts | Do not share outside the company |
| Confidential | Pricing, supplier contracts, recipes | Need-to-know; encrypted in transit |
| Restricted | Employee personal data, payroll | Access approved by data owner; never in AI tools |

## Using AI tools
- Only approved AI tools (including SnackBot) may be used with Internal or Confidential data.
- **Restricted data must never be entered into any AI tool.**
- AI-generated content must be reviewed by a human before it is shared externally.

## Reporting incidents
Report phishing or suspected security incidents to **security@crunchwell.example** or the IT
hotline (ext. 4444) within **1 hour** of discovery.

## Devices
Laptops must have full-disk encryption and lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
